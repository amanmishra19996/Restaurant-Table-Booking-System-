Our Story

Crafted with
Conviction

Aurum began as a single table in a borrowed kitchen. Chef Marcus Beaumont, trained under the Michelin-starred kitchens of Lyon and Tokyo, returned home with one conviction: exceptional food belongs to everyone, not only to the privileged few.

Today, our 40-seat dining room has become a destination — not for its celebrity, but for what happens at the table. Honest conversation, unhurried meals, and the kind of food that stays with you long after the last course.
